What is running

First, clean teachers demonstrate braking and steering on Sunset Speedway and Titan Loop. Three corrective imitation rounds teach students at the states they actually visit. Equal-budget baseline PPO and taught PPO are compared with three seeds. Race and Shootout have separate weights.

A package is ready only after both specialists pass 500 held-out attempts, a 97% lower reliability confidence bound, matched pace and racecraft checks, the 26 targeted cases and a recorded 50-race memory test. Attempt counts do not certify a model. After the pilot passes: all eight Extreme packages, then Hard, Medium, Easy and Ultra easy.

Local Python/CUDA training continues when this page or ChatGPT closes. Keep the laptop plugged in and awake. Failed checks keep the earlier driver. Qualified packages load automatically for AI Race and One Lap Shootout; Legacy remains a backup. Initial coverage: dry weather, stock performance, medium tyres.
