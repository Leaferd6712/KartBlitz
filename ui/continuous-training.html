What this campaign does

Eight tracks, five difficulties. Finish Extreme across all eight tracks first, then Hard, Medium, Easy and Ultra easy. Each model learns solo pace and full AI Races, reaches its attempt targets and passes native quality checks before it loads into the local game. Later-difficulty checkpoints and recordings stay saved and resume when their turn arrives.

Battery carries between race laps. DRS is automatic. Jobs take turns in parallel within the current difficulty. No deadline. Local training makes no paid AI/API calls and keeps running if chat credits reset or this window closes. Keep the laptop plugged in. Checkpoints and complete-attempt recordings are saved locally; restart the command below after a reboot.

npm run official:continuous

Only candidates passing all native approval cases become the default for AI Race and One Lap Shootout. A failed check retains the previous driver. This is measured improvement, not a guarantee of WR pace. Current coverage is dry weather, stock performance and medium tyres.

Track and difficulty coverage

Each model needs at least 10,000 completed training attempts, including failed attempts, plus full-race experience and a passing native-game evaluation. Counts persist across chunks and restarts. Interrupted fragments and evaluation laps do not count.
