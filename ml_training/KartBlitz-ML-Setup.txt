KARTBLITZ ML TRAINER - TRANSFER AND SETUP
=========================================

WHAT THIS TRAINS
- One neural driver for all KartBlitz circuits
- 24 track-relative sensors and continuous steering/throttle/brake
- A fixed 10% imitation warm-start followed by 90% PPO reinforcement learning
- Curriculum training with randomized kart physics and starting situations
- Five evaluation runs per track plus real checkpoint trajectories

ON THE NVIDIA TRAINING COMPUTER
1. Install Python 3.11 or newer from https://www.python.org/downloads/
2. Extract KartBlitz-ML-Trainer.zip into a writable folder.
3. Open PowerShell in the extracted KartBlitz folder.
4. FIRST TIME ONLY - RUN THIS NOW:
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\ml_training\install.ps1
5. Confirm the final line says "CUDA available: True".
6. EVERY TRAINING SESSION - RUN THIS NOW:
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\ml_training\start.ps1
7. Keep the terminal open. Copy its URL and six-digit pairing code.

FROM THE KARTBLITZ ML LAB
1. Open Controls & Settings and turn ML Lab ON.
2. In Build, adjust reward weights. Each slider explains its trade-off.
3. Optional: click DO NOW: RECORD A LAP and complete a clean lap. Human examples
   replace part of the built-in teacher's 10% allowance. They never reduce PPO's 90%.
4. In Train, enter the trainer URL and pairing code, then click DO NOW: CONNECT.
5. Choose Lesson (300k), Competitive (3m), or Endurance (10m).
6. Click DO NOW: START 90% PPO TRAINING. Watch the imitation and PPO phases.
7. Inspect Watch and the per-track evaluation table.
8. Export Your Model as a .kartml.json portable backup.

COMMAND-LINE TRAINING
python -m ml_training.train --steps 3000000 --envs 48

WHAT --steps MEANS
The value is the total learning budget. A 3,000,000-step run contains:
- 300,000 imitation samples (10%) to teach a safe starting behaviour
- 2,700,000 PPO experience steps (90%) to improve through reward and trial/error

WITH EXPORTED HUMAN DEMONSTRATIONS
python -m ml_training.train --steps 3000000 --envs 48 --demo kartblitz-human-demonstrations.json

DEFAULT OUTPUT
ml_training\runs\player-model.kartml.json

WHAT THE IMPORTANT PARAMETERS MEAN
- Learning rate: update size. Too high is unstable; too low is slow.
- Future reward (gamma): how strongly later progress matters.
- Advantage smoothing (GAE lambda): bias/noise balance in PPO estimates.
- PPO clip range: limits policy change during one update.
- Exploration bonus: keeps controls varied while learning.
- Rollout horizon: experience steps per environment before an update.
- Parallel environments: CPU simulations collecting experience at once.
- Imitation epochs: passes over teacher and recorded human examples.

IMPORTANT
- CUDA accelerates neural-network updates. The lightweight parallel racing
  environments in this version run on CPU, so CPU performance still matters.
- Training rotates through every track in sim\tracks\bakes.json.
- A simulation-qualified result is not proof of human-level pace.
- Validate weights in the real game and compare median laps on every track.
- Older v1 models remain importable, but new training exports v2 continuous models.
